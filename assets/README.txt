Drop your real files in this folder using these exact names:

  photo.jpg    -> your hero photo (portrait works best, tall/vertical)
  resume.pdf   -> your résumé PDF (the "Download PDF" link points here)

Until photo.jpg exists, the page shows a gray placeholder automatically.
Until resume.pdf exists, the "Download PDF" link will 404 — that's expected;
it starts working the moment you add the file.
